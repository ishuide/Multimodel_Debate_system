Common startup risks:
- unrealistic deadlines leading to burnout and poor quality
- insufficient testing causing production failures
- lack of market validation before building
- overengineering features nobody asked for
- unclear MVP scope leading to feature creep
- single point of failure (one developer knows everything)
- no CI/CD pipeline causing deployment fear
- ignoring security until it's too late
- poor database design that doesn't scale
- choosing trendy tech over proven solutions

Technical risks by category:

AI/ML projects:
- training data quality and bias
- model accuracy below acceptable threshold
- high compute costs for inference
- cold start latency issues
- hallucination in LLM-based features
- data privacy and compliance (GDPR, CCPA)

Web/SaaS projects:
- scalability bottlenecks under load
- third-party API dependency failures
- authentication/authorization vulnerabilities
- poor mobile responsiveness
- slow page load times (>3 seconds = user loss)

Team risks:
- bus factor of 1 (critical knowledge in one person)
- skill gaps in critical areas
- communication breakdown in remote teams
- unclear ownership of components
- developer burnout from crunch culture

Market risks:
- building what nobody wants (42% startup failure reason)
- competitor launching first
- pricing model not sustainable
- target audience too narrow
- regulatory changes invalidating product

Mitigation strategies:
- validate MVP with 10 real users before scaling
- always have automated tests for critical paths
- deploy to staging environment first
- conduct weekly risk reviews
- maintain a risk register with severity ratings
- plan for 2x the estimated timeline for unknowns
